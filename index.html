Project overview

Turning product vision into actionable delivery

Swap Space was a multi-platform property technology solution bringing together the Buyer App, Agent App, Agent Web Platform and Admin Panel. As Technical Project Manager, I worked across Product, Design, Engineering, QA and Compliance to turn product requirements and designs into structured, actionable delivery.

One of the key challenges was coordinating multiple platforms while requirements and designs evolved. My approach was to create greater structure around delivery: Product Requirements → Figma → WBS → User Stories → Acceptance Criteria → Development → Testing → Release Readiness. This gave Product and Engineering clearer communication, and gave stakeholders better visibility into progress, dependencies and blockers.

  • Translating designsTurning requirements and Figma designs into WBS, epics, user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Breaking down workSplitting features into actionable frontend, backend and mobile tasks, and coordinating dependencies across platforms.
  • Tracking deliveryProgress, blockers, risks and priorities, kept visible to Product, Engineering and stakeholders.
  • Beyond the roadmapSupporting GDPR and compliance-related delivery, and coordinating testing, UAT and beta preparation.
01 — Buyer Mobile App · 02 — Property Matching

Making property discovery easier for buyers

The Buyer App was designed around the property discovery journey — giving users ways to explore properties, save properties, and receive property matches. My delivery focus was coordinating the breakdown of these experiences into development-ready requirements, and working with the relevant teams to keep the designs, requirements and implementation aligned.

01

Onboarding. Requirements for Apple, Google and email sign-in, broken down into development-ready stories.

02

Sign in. A lighter return path for people already on the platform, scoped and tracked through to release.

03

Home. Coordinated what earned a spot on the home screen — saved properties, offers, recent activity.

04

Buyer App — Property Matching. A focused property discovery experience designed to help buyers review and respond to potential property matches.

05

Buyer App — Saved Properties. Users can organise properties they're interested in and return to them during their property search journey.

03 — Agent Platform

Bringing property operations into one workspace

The agent platform brings together property management, listing requests, leads, inquiries, offers, tasks, team management and analytics — giving agents visibility into their day-to-day activity and property pipeline. My delivery focus was coordinating the breakdown of the platform into manageable workstreams, and working across frontend and backend teams to track dependencies, clarify requirements and monitor delivery.

01

Agent Web Platform — Operations Dashboard. A central workspace giving agents visibility across properties, leads, inquiries, offers and tasks.

02

Listing requests. Broken down into requirements so every request carries the buyer's contact and what they're after.

03

Offers. Tracked through the offer pipeline, from connected through to closed.

04

New offer. Scoped as a short, development-ready form agents can fill from a listing directly.

05

Inquiries. Booking requests queued with time and date, tracked through to resolution.
